Support

FiFi Recipes is the iPhone and iPad companion to fifi.cooking.

Contact

Email ios@fifi.cooking — we aim to reply within a few days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS or iPadOS version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the app's language?

Settings → Language. All 24 languages supported by fifi.cooking are available, including full right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Pashto, Hebrew, and Kurdish.

Does the app work offline?

The app streams recipe data from fifi.cooking, so an internet connection is required. Recently viewed recipes and images are cached on-device for a while, which speeds things up.

How does Kids mode work?

Kids recipes have a get-ready checklist (wash hands, apron, grown-up nearby), tickable ingredients, tool lists, big step-by-step cards, and an allergen banner. Steps marked for grown-ups are always flagged.

Why do some recipes show videos?

Some recipes link to Dr. Fatma's YouTube videos. They play inside the app via youtube-nocookie.com embeds, or you can open them in the YouTube app.

Something looks wrong in my language

Recipe translations come straight from fifi.cooking. Report the recipe name and language to ios@fifi.cooking and we'll fix the source data.
